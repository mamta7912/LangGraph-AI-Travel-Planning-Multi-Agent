## 5️⃣ Practical Tips for a Smooth Trip  

| Topic | Advice |
|-------|--------|
| **Weather** | December is the peak winter season – pleasant 22‑30 °C, low humidity. Pack light cotton, a light sweater for evenings, sunscreen (SPF 30+), sunglasses, and a hat. |
| **Packing List** | Swimwear, flip‑flops, comfortable walking shoes, a small day‑backpack, reusable water bottle, basic meds, power‑bank. |
| **Health & Safety** | Drink bottled water, avoid raw salads from street stalls (unless you trust the vendor). Carry a small first‑aid kit. |
| **Transport** | **Scooter rental** is the cheapest way to zip around (₹350 / day). Keep a copy of your driving licence and wear a helmet (mandatory). For night‑out, use a reputable taxi app (Ola/ Uber) – fares are transparent. |
| **Cash vs Card** | Most beach shacks accept cards, but keep **₹5,000–₹7,000 cash** for small vendors, tips, and entry fees. |
| **Connectivity** | Buy a **local prepaid SIM** (Airtel/Jio – ₹300 for 2 GB data + 30 days). |
| **Cultural Etiquette** | When visiting churches, dress modestly (cover shoulders & knees). Remove shoes before entering temples. |
| **Emergency** | Police: 100, Hospital (Goa Medical College): +91‑832‑242 5555, Tourist Helpline: +91‑932 222 2222. |
